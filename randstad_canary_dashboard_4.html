Every reported period back to 2019
Randstad only discloses absolute revenue and per-country margin in its half-year (Q2) and full-year (Q4) reports; Q1 and Q3 give growth rates only, so the series steps twice a year. Dark bars are Q2, light bars Q4; the line is EBITA margin (right axis). Hover any bar for revenue, margin, and organic YoY. Note Germany was already weak in 2019, well before the AI era. The last two panels are regional aggregates (Asia Pacific; Other Europe & Latin America) and only run from Q2 2023, the span on which those buckets are consistently defined.
